The |1h| 15-40bps "skip the medium-trend chop zone" gate is being FORWARD-tested on the DRY engine (scripts/multi_window_engine.py, DRY path only — PLACE/live untouched and still PAUSED).

**Why forward (not just backtest):** the gate looked good only on two POST-HOC slices (live 102, DRY 428) where I computed |1h| after the fact; a 7-day historical backtest (scripts/gate_oos_backtest.py) CONTRADICTED it (15-40 band won 100% there → gate cuts winners) but that backtest measured lead too close to settle (~99% win across all bands, disconnected from live's 74%) so it's uninformative. Only a clean forward test settles it. See [[er-and-bband-filters-fail-oos]].

**Implementation:** engine now computes |1h| move at ENTRY from Coinbase 1-min candles (`net1h_bps()`, cached 120s/coin), tags every DRY record with `net1h`, `tband` (0-15 / 15-40 / 40+), `gate_skip` (true if 15<=|net1h|<40). DRY still TAKES ALL trades (paper) so we keep the counterfactual — the proper design: record the gate decision live, observe all outcomes. Band is decision-time, not post-hoc → no slicing bias.

**Infra:** watchdog_dry.ps1 (background PowerShell) restarts DRY + token collector + copy_trade_probe if they die; checks every 180s; never launches --place. **DRY STOP-LOSS BUG FIXED 2026-06-05:** STOP_LOSS=-15 was NOT gated on PLACE, so DRY self-exited ("STOP-LOSS hit ... FINAL") whenever paper PnL hit -15 (fast in down regimes, esp. 15m at -4.7/loss) — THIS is why DRY kept dying mid-collection. Now `STOP_LOSS=(-15 if "--place" else -1e9)` → DRY never self-exits, collects continuously.

**15m ADDED 2026-06-05:** flipped `ONLY_5M=(PLACE)` so DRY now samples BOTH 5m AND 15m (live/PLACE stays 5m-only). First time 15m is forward-sampled at all — prior 15m view ("5m EV +8.1% > 15m +4.1% for our taker, but 15m raw win% higher") was BACKTEST-ONLY (scripts/backtest_5v15.py), zero live 15m data existed. Now both durations carry the |1h| gate labels, so one forward run answers BOTH: does the gate help, AND is 15m actually better than 5m for us. Compare by splitting on `"-5m-"` vs `"-15m-"` in slug.

**ENTRY-CUSHION label ADDED 2026-06-05 (more promising than the |1h| gate):** the punch-through analysis (scripts/punchthrough_backtest.py, 1s binance) found the reversal's VELOCITY does NOT separate win/loss (both ~4.8 bps/s) — the user's "reversal momentum" hypothesis refuted. What DOES separate: the ENTRY CUSHION = current |lead| above the settlement floor at entry. On the engine's true decision-time `lead` (DRY 449, 2 days): |lead|<8 (entered ~at the floor) = 65% win, EV −0.14; |lead|≥8 = 75%, +0.09 — and low<ok held on BOTH days (06-04 −0.07 vs +0.30; 06-05 −0.21 vs −0.07). More OOS-consistent than the |1h| gate (which failed the 7-day backtest). Cushion was ALREADY recorded as `lead`; added explicit `cushion`/`low_cushion` (|lead|<8) fields for clean forward comparison. CAVEAT: only 2 days, and |lead|<8 may be ~B-near-0.5 in disguise (proximity to settlement line); B-filters failed OOS before [[er-and-bband-filters-fail-oos]]. Forward-validate before trusting.

**FORWARD RESULTS @566 labeled records (2026-06-06, ~9h, one down-regime):** ① |1h| GATE = DEAD (0/3 samples): skipped band 15-40 (71%/EV-0.00) ≈ kept (70%/-0.06) — gate cuts the slightly-better band. Dropped for good. ② CUSHION (|lead|>=8) = VALIDATED: low<8 = 57% win / -0.43 EV (n=68) vs ok>=8 = 72% / -0.00 (n=498) — the ONLY entry filter to survive forward. DEPLOYED to multi_window_engine PLACE as `ENTRY_CUSHION=8.0` (skip |lead|<8 at entry). ③ 5m vs 15m FLIPPED: 15m now 80%/+0.10 > 5m 68%/-0.04 — CONTRADICTS the earlier 5m>15m backtest → regime-dependent, NOT stable, do not deploy. Stacking test: cushion alone lifts -0.05→breakeven; cushion+15m=+0.10 but that's the unstable 15m; adding the gate or B>=0.5 HURTS. "All filters" ≠ best — only cushion is robust. Even fully filtered, edge is thin (+0.10/order = $0.10/trade on 5 shares) and still in a down regime.

**EEBDE-ALIGNMENT label ADDED 2026-06-06 (strongest signal yet):** in-sample on recent overlap (DRY ∩ eebde value-favorite, n=93): when our side AGREES with eebde's value-favorite = 88% win / +0.44 EV (n=74); when we bet AGAINST his favorite = 42% win / -0.62 EV (n=19). So "skip when we'd bet against his favorite" looks like a stronger filter than cushion. Feasible real-time: he loads from ~5s, median load ~133s, we enter ~167s → his favorite is established before our entry, so we can check it live. Wired into multi_window_engine: `eebde_favmap()` polls his data-api activity (cached 15s), tags each DRY entry with `eeb_fav`/`eeb_aligned` (LABEL ONLY, no filter). Forward-validate the 88%-vs-42% before deploying. CAVEAT: n=19 against is small, recent single regime, and ties the self-taker to eebde being active + data-api latency. Timing finding: we are STRUCTURALLY LATE vs eebde (he early/continuous from 5s, we one-shot at 167s, +42s after his load midpoint) → our positive slippage is mechanical, not fixable by tuning.

**ACCEL filter ADDED to live self-taker 2026-06-06:** decoding eebde's HEAVY-bet trait — he bets heavy when the lead is CLEAR + STRENGTHENING (accel = lead-now minus lead-early-in-window > 0) + CLEAN; heavy bets win 97% vs light 64%. Backtested on 604 real DRY windows (with B/outcome): adding accel>0 to the self-taker (max_lead>=15 & |lead|>=8) lifted 75%/+0.06 → 83%/+0.21 EV; choppiness(flips) added nothing (so NOT added — avoided over-engineering like rate15/his_avgpx). Deployed accel>0 to multi_window PLACE; recording `accel` in DRY+PLACE for forward validation. CAVEAT (per [[no-conclusions-without-longrun-and-they-expire]]): in-sample-ish (reconstructed on the DRY windows), one ~21h regime, provisional — forward-validate before trusting. Mechanism: strengthening lead avoids the 'strong late spike that reverses' losses (the 17% windows where our momentum was 90% wrong vs eebde).

**SHARP-FLIP labels ADDED to DRY 2026-06-06 (from maker-loss analysis):** the 4 maker losses (see [[maker-not-real-tested]]) clustered at WEAK entry leads (|lead| 8.4 vs winners 11.3) and were reversal/whipsaw windows; user asked whether a SHARP reversal (lead leads one way, flips, rebuilds on the new side) is itself a high-quality entry signal worth trading the new side (NOT a hedge — hedging both sides of a binary = locked loss, px_A+px_B>1, proven by maker window-1 −$2.35). Engine now tracks directional extremes `peak_up`/`peak_dn` (never reset) + `last_flip_t`; each DRY entry tagged with `flip_swing`(peak_up−peak_dn), `old_side_peak`(how strong the opposite side got before flipping to entry side), `since_flip`(secs since last sign flip = reversal freshness), `flips`, and `sharp_flip` (bool: flips>=1 & |old_side_peak|>=8 & |lead|>=8 & since_flip<=90). LABEL ONLY, no filter (DRY collects). Forward-validate: do `sharp_flip`-tagged entries win MORE (reversal=real signal) or LESS (reversal=chop) than non-flip entries, and at what entry price. Per [[out-of-sample-catches-overfit]]: do NOT mine a filter from the 4 losses — wait for forward sample.

**ACCEL>=10 THRESHOLD = OVERFIT, REJECTED 2026-06-07.** The 0.29-day accel-labeled DRY data (n=73 btc/eth+cushion) showed a clean monotonic win%↑ and accel>=10 cum EV +0.22 — looked like the strongest signal ever, I nearly recommended raising ENTRY_ACCEL 0→10. User wisely asked to reconstruct accel from binance on MORE history. Reconstructed (lead@60 vs lead@~150s) on 1080 5m btc/eth settled trades / 2.5 days (scripts/accel_recon_hist.py): win% DOES rise with accel (65→81%, real signal) BUT **EV stays ~flat/negative** — higher accel = stronger lead = higher B (priced-in), win% gain competed away. With cushion: cum EV >0=+0.02, >5=+0.04, **>10=+0.00 (NOT +0.22), >15=−0.05, >20=−0.05** — sweet spot is MODERATE accel (5-10 bucket +0.14), and EXTREME accel turns negative (climax/blow-off reversal, matches the lead+37/accel+33 losers). So accel>=10 was small-sample/regime overfit; big sample says don't raise the threshold. KEEP accel>0 (cum +0.02 w/ cushion ≈ breakeven). Caveat: reconstructed accel ≠ live accel exactly (binance vs Coinbase, early-capture timing) so part of the gap may be reconstruction noise — but conclusion (don't raise) is safe either way. Textbook [[out-of-sample-catches-overfit]]: monotonicity on 0.29d ≠ holds on 2.5d. Lesson reinforced: even a clean monotonic dose-response on a small sample can fail OOS; reconstruct on days before trusting a threshold.

**How to apply / next step:** after ~1-2 days (target: a few dozen `gate_skip:true` settled records), compare win%/EV of gate_skip=true vs gate_skip=false in data/multi_window_engine.jsonl (filter records that have `tband`). Gate is REAL only if skipped trades genuinely won less / were -EV AND it holds across days. If skipped trades won as much as kept (like the 7-day backtest), gate is dead like every other filter. Do NOT deploy to live until forward-validated. Caveat from history: every entry filter so far cut winners not losers OOS [[out-of-sample-catches-overfit]].
