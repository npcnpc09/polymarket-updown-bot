**Do NOT hand the user the conclusion "maker can't capture eebde's cheap fills / maker is -EV for us" as if it's established. It is NOT.** (User pushed back on this 2026-06-06 and was right.)

What we actually have on maker:
- **winner_skew (-9%) and hedge_maker (-14%)**: both OBSERVE-ONLY PAPER probes using an ASSUMED fill model. That is exactly the FILL-MODEL TRAP I warn about for OPTIMISTIC sims (e.g. the +46pp backtest) — but I was TRUSTING it when it gave PESSIMISTIC numbers. Double standard. A paper fill model can be wrong in either direction; it proves nothing about real fills.
- **two_sided_arb_v7**: REAL money but only a few trials (tiny) — not conclusive.
- **A real-money maker test at meaningful scale/duration measuring our ACTUAL fill ratio (do we fill on winners or get adverse-selected): NEVER DONE.**

**Why:** the whole crux of whether we can replicate eebde (buy the 80%-priced-in favorite cheaper as a maker = his +8c edge) hinges on the real fill ratio, which ONLY a real resting-order probe can measure. Paper assumes it.
