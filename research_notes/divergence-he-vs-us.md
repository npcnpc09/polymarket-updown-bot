Studying "we traded, eebde didn't — why he didn't" (user ask 2026-06-06). Two findings + the infra.

**Two-way comparison (self-taker DRY-pass ∩ eebde copy data, recent ~21h, small n):**
- **WHY WE skip HIS heavy bets** (scripts/why_we_skip.py, 112 eebde>=1500 windows): 48% we'd also enter; **36% blocked by our max_lead>=15** (his sweet spot is peak-lead 8-20, we cut the 8-15 half) — and that skip is likely CORRECT: 8-15 is his MAKER-cheap zone = −EV for our TAKER (priced-in), not a missed opportunity; 8% wrong-direction (he's genuinely right there), 6% accel<=0, 2% cushion.
- **WHY HE skips OURS** (scripts/two_way_monitor.py, our DRY-pass ∩ his data n=61): his bet SIZE gives us NO info — he-heavy 74% vs he-light 74% win (our own filter already extracted it). The ONLY useful eebde signal is **DIRECTION**: when his value-favorite is OPPOSITE our side (n=13) we win just **31% / EV −0.75** (re-confirms eeb_aligned 88%-vs-42% in [[gate-1h-forward-test]]). So "he didn't load" ≠ danger; "he loaded the OTHER side" = danger.

**cid limitation (why history can't be mined):** data-api /trades needs conditionId; cid is only resolvable for RECENT windows (gamma slug-lookup returns empty for old updown slugs; /activity offset-capped ~3000 ≈ his last ~50min only, he does ~490 trades/10min). So per-trade reconstruction of OLD windows is impossible. Deep-dive on the 2 windows reachable live: both he went light/opposite exactly when EARLY LEAD was weak (~5bps) or CHOPPY (lead flip-flopping) — consistent with his continuous-read cleanliness filter — but n=2 and we won both (coin-flip luck), proves nothing.

**OUTCOMES ARE STREAKY, not mean-reverting (2026-06-07) — drives the maker restart gate.** User proposed "after consecutive DRY losses the next is more likely to win (probability evens out)". Tested (scripts/loss_streak_test.py, 1475 windows ordered by time): REFUTED, and the OPPOSITE holds — after 0 losses (prev win) next-window EV +0.13 / win 76%; after 1 loss −0.45; after 2 −0.22; after 3+ losses **EV −1.31 / win 46%**. And after wins: 1→−0.03, 2→+0.15, 3+→+0.19. So our win/loss sequence is POSITIVELY autocorrelated (loss→more loss, win→more win) because losses happen in choppy regimes that PERSIST (matches the COLD-persists regime test). On independent events "due for a win" is the gambler's fallacy; on our regime-driven sequence it's backwards. USEFUL INVERSE SIGNAL: open the maker when recently WINNING (good regime persists), pause when recently LOSING. VOL GATE REPLACED: vol<5 was wrong (vol predicts win-rate=priced-in not EV; the $59→$113 profit run was HIGH-vol 7-16, vol<5 would've blocked it — and richer hourly analysis showed NO market feature (vol/efficiency/net/autocorr) robustly predicts our EV; profitable run was marginally more directional but weak). New maker gate (check_suitable.py + regime_guard.py loss_streak): SUITABLE iff regime!=COLD AND loss_streak<2. See [[gate-1h-forward-test]], [[er-and-bband-filters-fail-oos]].

Flaw caught live: maker bled real money (+$26→+$0.6, wallet→$60.8) while DRY showed win_streak=10/GOOD — because maker bids WEAKER/EARLIER than DRY measures (lead>=5 at 30-120s, both sides, 5m+15m vs DRY's |lead|>=8 @167s single), so "DRY warm" != "maker winning" and the DRY-based stop never fired. Fix (regime_guard.py maker_loss_streak from early_maker_live.jsonl recent <12min windows; watchdog_dry.ps1): START = DRY win_streak>=2 (only live signal while maker idle) AND 600s cooldown since last stop; STOP = maker's OWN recent loss_streak>=2.
