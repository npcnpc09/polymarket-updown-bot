- **MECHANISM**: over a 15m window the favorite is priced UP to reflect its higher (longer-horizon) certainty → the 96-97%/85% win rate is PRICED IN, same as our 0.95-band dead zone ([[winrate-not-profit-only-edge-is-buy-below-fair]], [[profit-map-barbell-and-blind-benchmark]]). High win rate × expensive price = breakeven.

- **ce25 confirms it too (real data by duration):** ce25 15m = **+0.1% ROI / 96% win** (2,338 mkts, $668k) vs his 5m +0.7%/93%. IDENTICAL to eebde's 15m +0.1%. TWO opposite-style whales (eebde=favorite-loader, ce25=cheap-underdog) BOTH net +0.1% breakeven on 15m despite 85-96% win → triple-confirmed the high win rate is priced in. (Bonus: ce25's 5m is only +0.7% ROI too — even the cheap-underdog whale has thin per-trade ROI; updown is a thin-margin market for everyone, profit = volume/execution, not fat per-trade edge.)
