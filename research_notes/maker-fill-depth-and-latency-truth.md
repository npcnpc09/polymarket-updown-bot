**QUEUE-POSITION → ADVERSE-SELECTION insight (user, 2026-06-22) — sharpens the latency case for the cell maker.** Mechanism: CLOB is FIFO price-time priority. Back-of-queue (our 103ms) means our resting bid ONLY fills when a sell sweep is BIG enough to clear everyone ahead of us — and a big sweep = a REVERSAL = the adverse fill. Front-of-queue (low latency) fills on the FIRST small noise-pullback sells = the GOOD fills. So a LOW fill rate (high latency) is NOT "fewer but equal-quality" fills — it's "fewer AND adverse-selected" (we get the reversal-sweep fills, the fast players took the good noise pullbacks first). Corollary: lowering latency improves BOTH fill RATE and fill QUALITY (catch good pullbacks, not just reversal sweeps) → could genuinely raise the cell win%/G, not just volume. This REVERSES my earlier "more fills might be more adverse" qualifier.
