**Entry-timing studied two ways (2026-06-16, scripts bt_5m_timing.py + bt_5m_timing_price.py). This is the throughline of the whole 5m engine — the eth ~$60 DRY-vs-live swing was pure early-vs-late execution.**

**PART 1 — win% vs entry minute (17,280 5m windows / 30d, binance-only, price-independent):** win% rises MONOTONICALLY with waiting. |lead|>=8: min1(60s) 86% → min2 92% → min3(180s) 97% → min4 100%. With z>=2.5: min1 95% → min3 99.5% → min4 100%. The gain is REAL loser-filtering, not sample-shrink: of windows firing at min1, commit→86% but wait-to-min3→97.5%; **88% of early-commit losers are eliminated by waiting** (they flip 10% or fade below the gate by min3; only 12% persist). Opposite of the overfit tell — waiting cuts LOSERS not winners. Validates the z-maker LATE_FRAC=0.55 (~min3) empirically at scale.

**PART 2 — the buy-below-fair EDGE = win% − PM price (700 real PM-priced 5m windows):** for a STRONG z signal the edge is BIGGEST EARLIEST and shrinks as you wait, because the market reprices fast. z>=2.5: **min1 96.9%win @0.785 = +18.4pp** → min2 +10.3 → min3 99.3%@0.924 = +6.9 → min4 100%@0.963 = +3.7. The signal is already ~97% at min1 but the market has only priced 0.785; by min3 win% barely moves yet price catches to 0.924. The gap (our edge) is largest before the market reacts.

**RECONCILIATION (why both parts are true) = the maker/taker split:** TAKER can capture the early edge (cross the spread at min1 before reprice → 96.9%win × 0.785). MAKER is FORCED late — at min1 price is rising so a resting bid below fills only on the windows about to reverse (adverse selection); only by min3, when price stabilizes ~0.924, can a maker bid fill without adverse selection. This EXPLAINS: why we run both engines; why the z-maker is late; and why the CELL (0.90-0.95) lives exactly at min3 (buying the now-97%-certain favorite at 0.92 before price fully converges to 0.99 at min4). The edge = (true win%) > (market price), maximized late-but-not-too-late for the maker.

**CONFIRM-TIMING sub-study — 15s confirm VALIDATED, do NOT shorten (2026-06-16, scripts/analyze_confirm_timing.py, 90 confirmed entries + 127 faded-spikes w/ traj, ~2 days since 06-15 13:00).** The early_taker waits CONFIRM_SEC=15 after a lead-cross before entering; the traj instrumentation sampled lead+ask each tick during that wait. COST of waiting: ask drifts **+3.18c** cross→confirm (59/90 rose) — the favorite gets priced up (micro-version of the early-edge effect; ask is FLAT the first ~5s then climbs). On the windows that DO confirm, entering earlier is cheaper: EV/sh T5=+0.118 / T8=+0.110 / T10=+0.100 / T15=+0.086 (win% 88.9% all, same windows). BUT you can't cherry-pick the will-confirm ones — shortening re-admits the FADED spikes the confirm filters: 127 fades win only **62.2%** (= −0.14/sh at ~0.77 ask, strongly −EV); T5 admits 79 fades@63%, T8 admits 58@60%. Net TOTAL profit: **15s=+7.74u >> 10s=+2.2u >> 5s=−0.44u** — shortening trades more but each added trade is −EV, dragging to breakeven/negative. The 15s confirm's fade-filtering value EXCEEDS the 3c ask-drift cost → KEEP 15s, a rare "param already calibrated, don't touch" (echoes [[signal-stop-doubles-roi]]: the lever is entry QUALITY/fade-filtering, not micro-timing). NEXT: z may filter fades FASTER than waiting (z = significance at the cross instant; confirm = persistence over 15s) — if the taker z-log shows fades have low z, then z-gate + shorter confirm could beat 15s-confirm-alone. The z-logging (added same day) will test this. Caveat: n=90/127, ~2 days, one regime.
