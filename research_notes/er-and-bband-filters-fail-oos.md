Tested two more entry filters to fix the updown taker drawdown ($112 peak → dip $80 → recovered $90 via redemption lag). BOTH fail out-of-sample, same as persist/macro/LLM/ML before them.

**ER-gated trend alignment (the "catch big sustained trends" idea):** backtested 589 btc/eth 5m windows over 5 days. Baseline (bet min3-leading side) = **97% win, stable 93-100% per day**. Counter-trend bets (side≠1h macro) ALSO win 97%; high-ER counter-trend (ER 0.3-0.5) win 92%. Skipping counter-trend-in-trend only cuts 13/589 trades and those WON 92% — cutting winners again. **Direction is NOT the leak: leading side wins ~97% regardless of ER, macro, or counter-trend status.**

**B-price-band filter:** live 102 real orders said B<0.70 entries = 46% win, −EV (the "leak"); B≥0.80 = 96% win, +$9.75. BUT DRY 428 btc/eth orders CONTRADICT: B 0.60-0.70 is the BEST band (+0.43/order), B 0.70-0.80 is the WORST (−0.15). The two samples flip sign in the mid/low bands. Only B≥0.90 is consistent (+EV, tiny). So the live "B<0.70 is the leak" was recent-downtrend-regime small-sample noise, not structure.

**Why:** the market is well-calibrated. The favorite's win prob is priced into the ask (certainty is priced-in, see [[maker-price-band-sweet-spot]]). No direction- or price-selection filter robustly beats take-all because there's no mispricing to select on. The real loss mechanism is structural: as a TAKER we start every trade underwater (spread + favorite asymmetry: win small +$1.1, lose big −$2.8), and multi-window correlation clusters the rare tail losses into batches — NOT wrong direction (we pick right ~97%).

**LIVE regime-flip proof (2026-06-05, the $59→$113→$90 arc):** split the 102 live orders at the equity peak (trade #77, 11:10). The SAME counter-trend/bounce-buy bets did: PRE-peak (+26 run-up) counter-trend 82% win +$14.9; DRAWDOWN (−19 bleed) counter-trend 50% win −$14.7. The bounce-buying that BUILT the peak is the exact same bet that bled it — regime flipped mean-reverting→trending with no warning. A "skip counter-trend" filter would cut the +14.9 winners to avoid the −14.7 losers = net ~zero. This is WHY persist/macro/ER all fail OOS, shown live in one session. Mechanism: our signal is momentum (buy what just rose) → at turning points it buys local tops (e.g. 11:20 bought BTC/ETH at +34/+56 spike top, B=0.94, then reversed down). Momentum is structurally counter-trend AT reversals. Verdict: strategy is structurally breakeven — wins in ranging regimes, bleeds in trending; calibrated market makes them cancel. User was RIGHT the drawdown was directional (down-dominated, Up bets bled); the point is it's unfilterable because the same bet prints in the other regime.

**MULTI-FACTOR also fails OOS — the alpha is PM ORDER FLOW, not momentum (2026-06-07):** user pushed to build a CONTINUOUS multi-factor judgment (like eebde) instead of single thresholds. Did it right: (1) B<0.40 deep-underdog per-day — flips sign across days (+EV on 06-05, −EV on 06-06), internally non-monotonic (B0.25-0.33 +0.22 vs B0.33-0.40 −0.46 = noise). (2) pure-python logistic P(win) model on [B, cushion, maxLead, accel] TRAINED 06-04/05, TESTED 06-06/07 (scripts/multifactor_oos.py): learned sensible weights (cushion+0.45, maxLead−0.34=climax-reversal hurts, accel+0.19) and looked +EV in-sample (rule P_pred>B = +0.15) but **OOS was −0.35, WORSE than take-all baseline −0.15**. Definitive: B/cushion/max_lead/accel are ALL underlying-momentum/price-derived and the market prices them efficiently — no STABLE incremental alpha, so single OR multi-factor combos overfit. eebde's edge is NOT a function of these momentum factors; it lives in data we never fed the model: **PM ORDER FLOW (book depth, aggressor side, bid/ask imbalance) + maker execution**. The ONLY unexplored feature space = the PM book snapshots the engine already records (DRY `snaps`: up/dn bid/ask at min2/3/4). Also accel>=10 threshold = overfit (0.29d monotonic +0.22 → 2.5d reconstructed breakeven, [[gate-1h-forward-test]]).

**DAY-TREND SIDE-BIAS also fails OOS (2026-06-27, bt_daytrend_align.py):** user observed we lack a day-level direction signal (on a slow-up BTC day our btc-Down fills bled). Tested biasing the 5m/15m side toward the REALIZED past-4h/12h binance net move (1658 real btc/eth fills). IN-SAMPLE looked real: aligned (side==day-trend) +0.023 EV/sh / +$189 vs counter −0.017 / −$23. BUT HELD-OUT (last 40%) KILLED it: 4h-aligned EV→−0.013, 12h-aligned→−0.028, and 12h-counter flipped POSITIVE (+0.006). Same mechanism as the live regime-flip proof: trend-days punish counter-side, mean-reversion days reward it, they cancel → no stable directional alpha. CONCLUSION: we genuinely lack a day-direction signal AND it can't be filled (predict OR realized-trend-align both OOS-fail). Don't re-test trend-alignment again.

**How to apply:** STOP proposing entry filters (direction, ER, persist, macro, B-band, accel-threshold, LLM, ML, multi-factor-on-momentum, day-trend-align) — all proven OOS-unstable or priced-in-tiny. Do not re-derive them. The ONLY untested +EV avenue is eebde's actual mechanism: TWO-SIDED MAKER (earn spread, net-load favorite, +$56k/wk — see [[eebde-is-spread-maker-not-directional]]). It cannot be validated from min2/3/4 snapshots without real fill flow (that's the fill-model trap [[maker-price-band-sweet-spot]]); only a tiny live two-sided-maker probe can test it. Also: redemption lag (~5min) made the wallet look like it bled to $80 when it was really $90 — judge PnL on settled wallet only.
