When a signal or threshold is discovered/tuned ON a dataset, its backtest on that SAME data is optimistically biased — you fit the threshold to the noise. ALWAYS validate on a held-out (out-of-sample) set before trusting it, and especially before risking real money.

**Why (the concrete case, 2026-06-05):** `persist = |lead|/max_lead` (momentum retained at entry) was mined from the DRY multi_window data — the winners-vs-losers bucketing there showed persist monotonic (33%→82% win) and a `persist>=0.5` filter backtested as a clean improvement (+$27.40→+$30.90, win 76%→80%) ON THAT SAME DATA. Looked like real alpha. BUT validating on a DIFFERENT set — yesterday's real_maker_test (single-window) max_lead-era trades — `persist>=0.5` would have **cut 2 WINNERS (−$4.55) and 0 losers** (yesterday's 4 losses all had persist 0.53-0.87 = clean-momentum entries that reversed near settle, NOT faded entries persist targets). Live confirmed the mush: #2 eth persist=0.51 LOST, #3/#4 persist 0.96-0.97 correlated Down batch. Reverted after only a few real trades (ENTRY_PERSIST set 0.5→0.0 = filter OFF, still logged for ongoing DRY validation). Net real-money cost of the mistake: tiny, because we OOS-checked fast.

**Two failure modes persist revealed (neither caught by entry filters):** (1) FADED entry — bought after momentum collapsed (persist<0.4, today's −$6.30 batch) — persist DOES catch this; (2) CLEAN entry then late reversal — strong momentum at entry (persist 0.5-1.0) that flips near settle (yesterday's 4 losses, the maxLead-tail losses) — persist does NOT catch this, it's the irreducible fat tail. No single entry filter catches all loss types; stacking them all over-filters (cuts volume + winners). So the favorite-asymmetry reversal tail is reduced by small size + sample size, NOT eliminated by entry features. Mirrors the [[signal-stop-doubles-roi]] refutation (stop value depends on entry quality; in-sample looked great, broke down on independent samples).

**ASK-DEPENDENT signals can't be historically backtested (2026-06-05):** the "skip when PM strongly disagrees (B<0.40)" filter — where B = the real ASK we pay as taker — passed the live 2-day OOS check (cut a bucket that was −EV BOTH days 06-04/05; better than persist). To validate over MORE days I tried ce25-history backtest, but it FAILED as a test: historical B can only be the prices-history MID (no bid/ask in that series), and live B is the real ASK — different measures, not comparable (historical B<0.40-mid ≈ a higher live ask). Plus prices-history fill is optimistic. So the historical run showed B<0.40 as +EV (opposite of live) purely from the B-measurement mismatch, not a real regime flip. LESSON: a signal that depends on the realized fill price (ask/slippage) can ONLY be validated forward on live data (which logs the real ask); historical mid-price series can't substitute. Decision: B≥0.40 kept PAPER-ONLY (DRY already logs real ask B) until more live days accumulate; NOT deployed.

**How to apply:** (1) any threshold mined from data → backtest is in-sample-biased → hold out an independent slice (different executor, different day) and re-check BEFORE deploying. (2) The tell of overfit: filter cuts winners not losers on the held-out set. (3) Risk only tiny real money on a freshly-mined signal until OOS-confirmed; revert fast if OOS is negative. (4) Validated entry edge stays: btc/eth + max_lead≥15 alone ([[btceth-only-entry-edge]]). See [[maker-price-band-sweet-spot]] (backtest-trap), [[signal-stop-doubles-roi]].
